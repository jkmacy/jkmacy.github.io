The Divine Office

Pray an Office at Orthodox West Breviary, or open its Ordo instructions for the Diurnal. Links use the device-local civil date of the scheduled Office, including after midnight. Vespers stays on its civil evening; the Breviary handles feast precedence. Matins uses the Ordo, and Compline’s Ordo link opens the day’s overview. Breviary links need an internet connection.

    Location & installation

    Use your device location or enter coordinates. Location stays on this device; calculations run on your device.

    North and east are positive; south and west are negative. Civil times and link dates use this device’s time zone, even when coordinates are elsewhere.

    Install from your browser’s menu when available.

    The day begins at first light (civil dawn), at 00:00, with Lauds. The time from first light to last light (civil dusk) is divided into twelve seasonal hours; last light is 12:00, with Compline. The night has twelve more hours, ending at 24:00 as the next first light resets the clock. Seasonal minutes and seconds stretch with their hour. The smaller local clock and countdown use ordinary time.

    Lauds begins at first light (00:00), Prime at sunrise, Terce at 03:00, Sext at 06:00 (approximately solar noon), None at 09:00, and Vespers at sunset. Compline begins at last light (12:00), and Matins halfway through the night at 18:00. These retain the app’s devotional pattern, not binding Benedictine rubrics.

    Today and Tomorrow refer to first-light-to-first-light days. Before first light, the current day still bears the previous day’s date. Times are approximate; terrain and weather affect visible dawn and sunset.
